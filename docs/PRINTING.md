# Printing

Recommended starting settings:

- Material: TPU
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Walls: 4
- Infill: 20%
- Supports: No
- Brim: 10 mm outer brim
- Orientation: Print vertically

Print the insert at 100% scale and confirm fit before use.
